Legend — DBI Land passing-listings export

Each row in the companion sheet is one farmland parcel that passed every hard-fail criterion. Higher score values are always better. _distance_m columns are raw meters (lower = closer = usually better).

Identity & link

Column Meaning
rank 1-based rank by total_score (sheet's natural order).
source Which scraper found it: landsearch, landwatch, landandfarm.
listing_id The source's own ID for this parcel.
url Direct link to the listing page — click to view.

Location & physical

Column Meaning
state, county Self-explanatory.
acres Parcel acreage. Targets: 350-800 ac (preferred), 801-1500 (ok), 1501-3000 (acceptable).
price_usd Asking price. Hard cap at $3,000,000 total.
price_per_acre Computed. Hard cap at $6,000/ac.
lat, lon Decimal degrees, geocoded from the listing's state+county via OSM Nominatim. NOT parcel-exact — usually the county centroid or nearest town, accurate to ~5-30 km. Use for regional sorting, not driving directions.
elevation_m USGS 3DEP ground elevation at (lat, lon), in meters.

GIS proximity scores (0.0 = far/bad, 1.0 = ideal)

All proximity scores are inverse-distance curves: at or below ideal_m → 1.0, at or above max_m → 0.0, linear ramp between.

Column Source Ideal / Max Notes
water_score USGS NHD perennial streams + springs (FCodes 46006, 45800) 200 m / 3 km Year-round water access. Hard-fail floor in criteria: min 0.2.
water_distance_m — — Raw meters to nearest perennial water feature.
power_score HIFLD transmission lines (≥69 kV) 50 m / 1.6 km Closer = cheaper grid hookup. NOTE: too close is also annoying (line easements over your land) — sort and use judgment.
power_distance_m — — Raw meters to nearest transmission line.
tower_score OpenStreetMap man_made=communications_tower / mast 50 m / 5 km Higher score = closer to cell coverage.
tower_distance_m — — Raw meters to nearest tagged tower. OSM coverage is patchy in rural areas, so a high distance may mean "no nearby tower OR no nearby tagged tower."
road_access_score — — Reserved column. No road-access enricher is wired yet, so this is usually blank.

Per-criterion score components

The total_score is a weighted average of these five. Each is in [0.0, 1.0]. A score of 0.0 on any one = hard-fail = the row was dropped (so for passing listings these are all > 0).

Column Weight (from criteria.yaml) What it scores
state_score 1.0 1.0 if state is on the target list (TX, OK, AR, MO, KS, IA, NE, MN, WI, IL, IN, OH, KY, TN, MS, AL, GA, NC, VA, PA, NY, NJ, MI, SD, ND, LA, FL, OR, WA), 0.0 otherwise.
acreage_score 1.5 Peaks at the midpoint of the matched band; falls off toward band edges. Multiplied by the band's own weight (1.0 / 0.9 / 0.75 for the three bands).
price_score 2.0 Headroom under the $/ac cap: 1 - price_per_acre / 6000. Cheaper = higher.
water_crit_score 1.5 Same as water_score above, just inside the criteria framework.
road_crit_score 1.0 Same as road_access_score (currently always at the floor since no enricher).

Computed totals

Column Meaning
total_score Weighted average of all five criterion scores. Sheet is sorted by this descending; higher = better fit to your full criteria.

Suggested sorts in the sheet

  • Cheapest viable land: sort price_per_acre ascending.
  • Biggest ranches: sort acres descending.
  • Best water access: sort water_distance_m ascending (or water_score descending).
  • Lowest infrastructure cost: sort power_distance_m ascending.
  • Most remote (off-grid feel): sort tower_distance_m descending, then filter water_score > 0.5.
  • Best overall fit: leave it on default total_score descending.

Caveats

  • lat/lon are geocoded centroids (county or nearest town), NOT parcel-exact. All proximity scores inherit that ~5-30 km regional fuzziness.
  • tower_distance_m reflects only OSM-tagged towers — rural under-tagging means a large number may mean "no nearby tower OR no one's mapped it yet."
  • Scrapers run weekly; a row's presence here only proves the listing existed on its last scrape (within the 14-day freshness window).
